# Professional Summary

## Formula

[Years of Experience] + [Specialization] + [Industries Served] + [Business Value]

---

## Example

Marketing Automation Specialist with 5+ years of experience helping e-commerce, finance, and coaching businesses automate customer journeys using ActiveCampaign, ManyChat, and Zapier. Passionate about building systems that improve efficiency, increase conversions, and create better customer experiences.

---

## Rules

✓ 3–5 sentences

✓ Focus on business value

✓ Mention specialization

✓ Mention industries when relevant

✗ No generic soft skills

✗ No objective statement
