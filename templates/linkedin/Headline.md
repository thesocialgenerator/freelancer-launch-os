# LinkedIn Headline

## Formula

[Specialization] | Helping [Target Audience] achieve [Business Outcome]

---

## Examples

Marketing Automation Specialist | Helping Service Businesses Generate More Leads Through AI & CRM Automation

UX Designer | Creating User-Centered Digital Experiences for SaaS Companies

Project Manager | Helping Agencies Deliver Projects On Time and On Budget

---

## Rules

✓ 220 characters or fewer

✓ Client-focused

✓ Outcome-driven

✓ Include specialization

✗ Generic job titles only

✗ Buzzwords
