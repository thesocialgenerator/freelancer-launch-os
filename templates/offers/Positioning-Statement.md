# Positioning Statement

## Formula

I help [Target Audience]

solve [Problem]

by [Solution]

so they can [Outcome].

---

## Example

I help injector clinics generate more consultations by building Instagram DM automation systems so they can convert followers into booked appointments.

---

## Rules

✓ One sentence

✓ Business outcome

✓ Client focused

✓ No buzzwords

✓ Easy to understand
