# Discovery Questions

## Purpose

Gather the minimum information required to build a compelling freelance offer.

---

## Business Goals

- What service do you want to offer?
- What outcome do you want to help clients achieve?

---

## Target Audience

- Who is your ideal client?
- What industry are they in?
- What size business do they have?

---

## Problems

- What challenges does your ideal client face?
- What happens if they don't solve this problem?

---

## Solution

- How do you solve this problem?
- What process or framework do you use?

---

## Experience

- What relevant experience do you have?
- Have you solved this problem before?

---

## Deliverables

- What will the client receive?

---

## Pricing

- One-time project?
- Monthly retainer?
- Hourly?
- Productized service?

---

## Success

What does success look like for your client?
