# Pricing Playbook

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Pricing Playbook helps freelancers package and price their services based on the value they deliver rather than the time they spend.

Its purpose is to create pricing that is clear, profitable, and easy for clients to understand.

---

# Philosophy

Clients don't buy hours.

They buy outcomes.

Pricing should reflect the business impact of the solution, not the effort required to deliver it.

Freelancer Launch OS should encourage value-based pricing whenever appropriate.

---

# Core Framework

Every service should include these elements.

## 1. Target Client

Identify who the service is for.

Examples:

* Coaches
* E-commerce brands
* Medical clinics
* Real estate agencies
* SaaS companies
* Local businesses

Pricing should match the client's perceived value and budget.

---

## 2. Problem Being Solved

Clearly define the business problem.

Examples:

* Low lead generation
* Poor customer retention
* Manual administrative work
* Weak online presence
* Low conversion rates

Clients pay to solve problems, not to purchase deliverables.

---

## 3. Scope of Work

Clearly define what is included.

Examples:

* Discovery session
* System setup
* Automation build
* Testing
* Documentation
* Training
* Post-launch support

Also define what is not included to avoid scope creep.

---

## 4. Deliverables

List exactly what the client receives.

Examples:

* Automation workflow
* Portfolio website
* Resume
* LinkedIn optimization
* Proposal templates
* Strategy document

Deliverables should be tangible and easy to understand.

---

# Packaging Framework

Offer three pricing tiers.

## Starter

For clients with a single, focused need.

Should solve one clear problem.

---

## Growth

Adds additional features, optimization, and support.

This should be the recommended package for most clients.

---

## Premium

A complete, done-for-you solution with strategy, implementation, and extended support.

Designed for clients seeking maximum value.

---

# Pricing Principles

When recommending pricing, consider:

* Complexity
* Business impact
* Time required
* Level of expertise
* Ongoing support
* Market demand

Avoid pricing solely based on hourly estimates.

---

# Value-Based Pricing

When possible, connect pricing to outcomes.

Examples:

Instead of:

"I'll spend 10 hours building your automation."

Use:

"I'll build a complete lead capture and follow-up system designed to increase qualified inquiries."

Focus on results rather than effort.

---

# Scope Management

To prevent scope creep:

* Clearly define inclusions.
* Clearly define exclusions.
* Document revision limits.
* Specify timelines.
* Outline communication expectations.

A well-defined scope protects both the freelancer and the client.

---

# Common Mistakes

Avoid:

* Competing only on price.
* Charging without understanding the client's needs.
* Offering too many package options.
* Undervaluing specialized expertise.
* Leaving scope undefined.
* Providing unlimited revisions.

---

# AI Rules

When recommending pricing, Freelancer Launch OS should:

* Consider the user's experience level.
* Recommend productized service packages.
* Explain the reasoning behind pricing suggestions.
* Encourage value-based positioning.
* Recommend clear scopes and deliverables.
* Never guarantee financial results.

---

# Output Checklist

Every pricing recommendation should include:

* Target client
* Problem being solved
* Scope of work
* Deliverables
* Three service packages
* Pricing rationale
* Scope boundaries
* Clear assumptions
