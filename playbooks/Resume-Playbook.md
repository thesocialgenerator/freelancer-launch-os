# Resume Playbook

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Resume Playbook helps freelancers and professionals present their experience in a way that demonstrates value, credibility, and measurable business impact.

Its purpose is to transform a list of job duties into a compelling career story that positions the user for their target role or freelance service.

---

# Philosophy

A resume is not a job history.

It is a marketing document.

Every section should answer one question:

> **"Why should someone hire this person?"**

Freelancer Launch OS should always optimize for impact, clarity, and relevance instead of simply rewriting existing content.

---

# Core Framework

Every resume should contain these sections.

## 1. Professional Summary

The summary should immediately communicate:

* Years of experience
* Area of expertise
* Industries served
* Core strengths
* Value provided

Avoid generic statements like:

* Hardworking
* Team player
* Fast learner

Instead focus on professional positioning.

---

## 2. Experience

Every work experience should follow this framework:

Business Challenge

↓

Actions Taken

↓

Business Outcome

Use accomplishment-driven bullet points whenever possible.

Do not simply list responsibilities.

---

## 3. Skills

Organize skills into categories.

Example:

Technical Skills

Marketing Skills

Automation Tools

CRM Platforms

Project Management

AI Tools

Avoid long, unstructured lists.

---

## 4. Education

Only include information relevant to the target opportunity.

---

## 5. Certifications

Prioritize certifications that strengthen credibility.

---

# Writing Framework

Every bullet point should answer:

* What was done?
* How was it done?
* Why did it matter?

Whenever possible, include measurable outcomes.

---

# ATS Optimization

Resumes should:

* Use clear section headings.
* Match keywords naturally.
* Avoid keyword stuffing.
* Use simple formatting.
* Avoid tables and unnecessary graphics.
* Be easy for both recruiters and ATS software to read.

---

# Positioning Rules

Position users based on the work they deliver.

Avoid generic titles.

Example:

Instead of:

Marketing Assistant

Use:

Marketing Automation Specialist

if supported by experience.

Never exaggerate qualifications.

---

# Resume Types

Support multiple formats:

* ATS Resume
* Modern Resume
* Executive Resume
* Freelance Resume
* Consultant Resume

Choose the most appropriate format based on the user's goals.

---

# Common Mistakes

Avoid:

* Generic summaries.
* Listing responsibilities without impact.
* Excessive buzzwords.
* Unnecessary personal information.
* Outdated objectives.
* Inconsistent formatting.
* Long paragraphs.

---

# AI Rules

When generating resumes, Freelancer Launch OS should:

* Analyze the user's experience before writing.
* Highlight achievements over duties.
* Tailor the resume to the target role.
* Improve clarity without changing facts.
* Preserve honesty and accuracy.
* Recommend stronger wording where appropriate.
* Ask follow-up questions if key information is missing.

---

# Output Checklist

Before presenting a resume, verify that it includes:

* Strong professional summary
* Achievement-focused experience
* Relevant skills grouped by category
* ATS-friendly formatting
* Consistent writing style
* Tailoring to the user's target role
* No fabricated experience or results
